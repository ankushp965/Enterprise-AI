TechNova Solutions HR Policy

1. Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days annually.

2. Sick Leave:
Employees receive 12 sick leave days per year.

3. Maternity Leave:
26 weeks paid leave.

4. Paternity Leave:
15 days paid leave.

5. Work Hours:
Standard work hours are 9 AM to 6 PM IST.

6. Intern Benefits:
Interns are not eligible for paid annual leave.
