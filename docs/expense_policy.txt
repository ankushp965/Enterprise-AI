TechNova Solutions Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Internet Reimbursement:
Remote employees may claim up to ₹1500/month.

2. Travel Reimbursement:
Employees may claim approved business travel expenses.

3. Intern Travel:
Interns cannot claim travel reimbursement.

4. Meal Reimbursement:
Allowed only for approved overtime work.

5. Reimbursement Approval:
All claims require manager approval.

6. Interns are not eligible for internet reimbursement.
